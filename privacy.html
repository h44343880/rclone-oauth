Privacy policy

This OAuth client is used only by its owner, with rclone running on the owner's own computer, to copy the owner's files to the owner's own Google Drive.

  • Google Drive access is used only to upload, list and compare the owner's backup files.
  • No data is collected, stored, sold or shared with anyone. Nothing is sent anywhere other than the owner's own computer and the owner's own Google Drive.
  • There are no other users. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page.

Questions: open an issue in this site's GitHub repository.
